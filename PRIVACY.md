# Poetry for the Day — Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026. Developed by Stephen Reitz.

## Information stored on your device

Poetry for the Day does not require an account. Favorites, reading history, reading streaks, cached poems, preferences, and local diagnostic events are stored on your device. The app shares selected poem data with its widget through shared local storage. Local diagnostic events are not sent to a developer-operated analytics service.

## Poetry and news requests

The app and widget request poems from PoetryDB. The daily-vibe feature can request public RSS news feeds to analyze current themes. These requests disclose ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to the service receiving the request. Those services operate under their own privacy practices. The bundled poetry collection can be read offline.

## AI features

AI poem generation and supported AI analysis use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. The app does not send your custom poem prompts to a developer-operated AI server. Availability depends on your device, operating system, and Apple Intelligence settings.

## Sharing and external links

Sharing a poem sends the content you choose through the app or recipient you select. Opening a source or license link connects you to the corresponding website. Those apps and websites have their own privacy policies.

## Advertising, tracking, and purchases

The app contains no advertising or third-party advertising tracking SDK. The developer does not sell your personal data. App Store purchases, optional Apple diagnostics, and device backups are handled by Apple under your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy.

## Your choices

You can manage favorites in the app and notification permissions in system settings. Deleting the app removes its app-container data; shared widget data or backups may remain subject to your device and backup settings. You can manage backups through Apple settings. Information you choose to share with another app or person is subject to their retention practices.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact Stephen Reitz through the project's support tracker: https://github.com/ssk42/Poem-of-the-Day/issues. Issues are public; do not include sensitive personal information. Information you voluntarily submit there is handled by GitHub and is used to respond to your request.

## Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
